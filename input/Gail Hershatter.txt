From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
American historian of Modern China
Gail Hershatter
is an American
historian
of Modern China who holds the Distinguished Professor of History chair at the
University of California, Santa Cruz
.
[
1
]
She previously taught in the history department at
Williams College
.
[
2
]
She graduated from
Hampshire College
with a B.A., from
Stanford University
with a M.A., and from
Stanford University
with a Ph.D. 
She was elected vice-president of the
Association for Asian Studies
in 2010
[
3
]
and subsequently elected president the following year.
[
4
]
She was an assistant director for the documentary
The Gate of Heavenly Peace
.
[
5
]
Her research interests include modern Chinese women's history and labor studies.
[
6
]
Her 2011 monograph,
The Gender of Memory
, uses the lens of rural women in
Shaanxi
Province, China, to examine revolutionary China in the 1950s and 1960s.
[
7
]
