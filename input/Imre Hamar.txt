From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Imre Hamar
is a professor of Chinese studies at
Faculty of Humanities
of the
Eötvös Loránd University
(
Budapest
). Hamar is also specialist in Huayan
Buddhism
.
[
1
]
He is also the president of
Budapesti EAC
.
Biography
[
edit
]
Born in Győr (Hungary), 1 April 1967. By 1992, he had MA degree of Eötvös Loránd University (Chinese and Tibetan).
[
2
]
Imre Hamar has been notably dedicated to the Chinese language teaching and research. Since 2006, he has been the director of the Confucius Institute at Eötvös Loránd University.
