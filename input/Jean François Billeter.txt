Jean François Billeter
Chinese name : 毕来德 / 畢來德 Bì Láidé, born 1939 in
Basel
,
Switzerland
, is a Swiss
sinologist
and honorary professor of the
University of Geneva
, where he created the sinology department in 1987. After directing it for twelve years, he retired in 1999 to spend more time writing.
