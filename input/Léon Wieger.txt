Léon Wieger
(born 9 July 1856 in
Strasbourg
,
France
- died 25 March 1933 in
Xian County
,
Hebei
,
China
), was a French
Jesuit
missionary, medical doctor, theologist and sinologist who worked at the Catholic Jesuit mission in
Hejian
, together with
Séraphin Couvreur
.
He published numerous books on Chinese culture, Taoism, Buddhism and the Chinese language.
