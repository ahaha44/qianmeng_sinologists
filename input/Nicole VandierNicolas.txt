From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
French sinologist and philosopher (1906 – 1987)
Nicole Vandier-Nicolas
Nicole Vandier-Nicolas
(24 July 1906 – 1 March 1987), also known as
Nicole Nicolas
, was a French
sinologist
, professor and
philosopher
.
[
1
]
She was specialized in
Chinese art
and
Buddhism
.
[
2
]
She was the “first to suggest the connection between the verse written on the one side of the
scroll
and the pictures on the other side.”
[
3
]
[
4
]
Biography
[
edit
]
Nicole Vandier-Nicolas was born as Alberte Émilie Marie Nicole Zoé Vandier on 24 July 1906 in Paris, France. She was a professor of Chinese civilization at the
National Institute for Oriental Languages and Civilizations
.  She also taught the subject of Chinese art at the
École du Louvre
. She translated a number of literary works from
Chinese
to
French
.
[
5
]
She died on 1 March 1987 in
Marcilly-d'Azergues
, France.
