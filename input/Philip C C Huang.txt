From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
American historian of China
Philip Chung-Chih Huang
is a historian of China who was for many years (1966–2004) a professor at the
UCLA
. While there, he founded there Center for Chinese Studies and he was the founding Editor of
Modern China: An International Journal of History and Social Science
.
