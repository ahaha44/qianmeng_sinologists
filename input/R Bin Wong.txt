Roy Bin Wong
(
Chinese
:
王國斌
;
pinyin
:
Wáng Guóbīn
; born 1949
[
citation needed
]
) is a Chinese
economic historian
at the
University of California, Los Angeles
.
He was the Director of the
UCLA Asia Institute
from 2004 to 2016.
[
1
]
He received his BA from the
University of Michigan
, and received his
MA
and
PhD
from
Harvard University
.
[
1
]
