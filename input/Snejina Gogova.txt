From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Linguistics researcher
Snejina Gogova
(
Bulgarian
: Снежина Гогова) (born 1937) is a
Bulgarian
Sinologist
,
sociolinguist
, and
psycholinguist
, and Professor of
Chinese Linguistics
at the Faculty of Classical and Modern
Philologies
of
Sofia University
.
[
1
]
Gogova published in English and Chinese between 1985 and 2001 and in Russian and Bulgarian from 1970 to 2009.
