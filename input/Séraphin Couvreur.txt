Séraphin Couvreur
(
Chinese
:
顧賽芬
;
pinyin
:
Gù Sàifēn
;
EFEO Chinese transcription
: kóu sái fēn; 14 January 1835 – 19 November 1919) was a French Jesuit missionary to China, sinologist, and creator of the
EFEO Chinese transcription
. The system devised by Couvreur of the
École française d'Extrême-Orient
was used in most of the French-speaking world to transliterate Chinese until the middle of the 20th century, after what it was gradually replaced by
pinyin
.

Hejian

Léon Wieger
(1856–1933), another French Jesuit missionary.
